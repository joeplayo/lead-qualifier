Team & permissions

User → Manager → Admin. Managers see their own descendants; users do not have parallel visibility.

Visibility model

Users see only assigned leads. Managers see leads owned by themselves and users/managers below them. Admins see the entire organization and can configure users, integrations, and automation.

Paid plans can use manager approval queues and external Hot-lead alert webhooks. Basic accounts stay on AI Autopilot with in-app human handoff.
